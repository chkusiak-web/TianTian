# 天天 Game Concept: The 73rd Spring

Oct 8, 2026 · @Moondog

天天 becomes an adventure game in pixel art: you explore four Chinese cities, one per HSK level, and solve each city's mystery by using the language. Drills, stories and conversations become the steps of the story, and your standing as a local is your health.

## The four cities

Each HSK level is its own city, like a region in Pokémon, with its own mystery, cast of 10, local status and passport.

| City | HSK level | New words | Feel | Culture woven in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 济南 Jinan | 1 | about 500 | Small, warm, neighborly | Springs, breakfast, grandparents |
| 成都 Chengdu | 2 | +772 | Relaxed, food-loving | Teahouses, hotpot, mahjong, pandas |
| 上海 Shanghai | 3 | +973 | Fast, modern | Metro, work, the Bund, shopping |
| 北京 Beijing | 4 | +1,000 | Grand, historic | Hutongs, history, formality |

- **Travel:** by train from 济南西站 (Jinan West Station). The next city stays locked until the current one is finished.
- **Cast:** each city has its own 10 people. Friends from earlier cities make cameo visits and phone calls.
- **Old words keep coming back:** Jinan words appear in Chengdu, so earlier vocabulary is reviewed in new contexts.

## Jinan's mystery: the 73rd spring

You inherit your great-uncle's courtyard house on 曲水亭街 (Qushuiting Street), and his notebook: a lifelong search for a 73rd spring that isn't on Jinan's famous list of 72 (七十二名泉).

- **The notebook is the progress bar.** It's written in Chinese and addressed to you. Pages become readable as you finish districts.
- **Every clue uses only HSK 1 words.** Difficulty climbs through length and complexity, not vocabulary:
  - Early clues are single words and short signs: 七十三, 红色, 四号.
  - The middle brings handwritten notes, ledgers and short letters.
  - The finale is the notebook's last page: a full letter written only in HSK 1 words. Reading it fluently is the final exam for Jinan.
- **The 10 characters** are helpers, witnesses and the occasional suspect. Grandma Wang knew him, Teacher Zhang has the history, Old Pan drove him everywhere.
- **The twist:** he always knew where the spring was. The notebook was a trail he laid so that whoever came after him would have to meet the neighbors, learn the language and become a local.
- **Finale:** at the Mid-Autumn Festival (中秋) the street gathers in your courtyard. The sealed well is opened, water rises, and 泉 (Quán), his first love, names it. You're 老济南 (Old Jinan).
- **Hook into Chengdu:** among 泉's letters is one from a friend at a Chengdu teahouse: “他还在等你的回信。” (He's still waiting for your reply.)

## The ten district arcs

All 10 districts teach HSK 1, about 50 words each, unlocking in order; each arc takes about a week at 15 minutes a day.

| # | District | HSK 1 theme | Grammar focus | Arc (the hook) | Clue gained |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 趵突泉 Baotu Spring | Greetings, people, names, countries | 是 · 叫 · 吗 · 呢 | Grandma Wang's lost thermos | His photo of a broken stele carved 七十三 (73) |
| 2 | 芙蓉街 Furong Street | Food and drink | 要 · 想 · measure words (个 / 碗 / 杯) | Master Sun's stolen 油旋 recipe | “My tea tastes best with *that* water” |
| 3 | 大明湖 Daming Lake | Time, days, weather | 点 / 分 · 今天 / 明天 · 很 | A boat sailing at the wrong times | His map, with arrows into the old alleys |
| 4 | 泉城广场 Quancheng Square | Money, numbers, colors, clothes | 多少钱 · 太……了 · 几 | A forged price tag at the market | You buy back his brass key (first big purchase) |

Behind the scenes, a coverage tracker checks that every HSK 1 word has a home in some arc: a person, place or object where you first meet it and later use it.

## Arc template and worked example

Every district runs the same five beats, and every drill has an in-world reason. Drills are framed as story actions but labelled, for example “Listening · find the pavilion”.

1. **Hook:** a neighbor has a problem.
2. **Investigate:** 3–5 clues, each one a labelled drill.
3. **Encounter:** a face-battle against whoever is in the way.
4. **Resolution:** the problem is solved and the neighbor shares what they know about your great-uncle.
5. **Payoff:** a notebook page becomes readable and the district's springs join your collection.

**Example: Baotu Spring, “Grandma Wang's thermos”**

| Step | In the story | Practice underneath |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | Grandma Wang is upset at the spring: “我的杯子没有了！” | Listening |
| Clue 1 | Ask the tai chi group if anyone saw it; Teacher Zhang thinks a boy picked it up | Greetings, names, 是 / 叫 / 吗 |
| Clue 2 | Find the right entry in Ms. Chen's handwritten lost-and-found ledger: 红色 · 杯子 · 上午九点 | Reading: colors, times, numbers |
| Clue 3 | A kid saw the boy at “四号亭”; mishear 四 as 十 and you search the wrong pavilion, costing face | Tones and listening, with consequences |
| Clue 4 | Find the pavilion using the park's signs | Reading 东 / 西 / 南 / 北, 左 / 右 |
| Encounter | The boy won't hand it over without a riddle duel | Mixed review |
| Resolution | “你叔公每天早上都来这儿……” (Your great-uncle came here every morning…) | Notebook page 1 |

Daily review becomes “refreshing your memory” before you talk to people: due words show up as faded notes in the notebook.

## Health: face and local status

Health works on two layers: 面子 (face) inside each encounter, and 本地 (local status) as your long-term level.

**面子 Face, per encounter**

- Each conversation or challenge starts with 5 hearts.
- A misunderstanding, wrong tone, broken measure word or English slip costs a heart; a natural, correct line or a local phrase restores one.
- At zero you retreat (“不好意思……我先走了”) and can try again later. Nothing is lost permanently.
- Tea (茶) bought with yuan restores face mid-encounter.

**本地 Local status, long-term**

| Tier | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| 游客 Tourist | People speak slowly and simply; vendors overcharge you |
| 新来的 Newcomer | Neighbors greet you by name |
| 邻居 Neighbor | Teacher Zhang trusts you with the courtyard well; Old Pan gives you rides (fast travel) |
| 熟人 Familiar Face | Local prices; the members-only teahouse; the neighbors' support for the finale |
| 老济南 Old Jinan | City complete; Chengdu opens |

- **It rises** from what locals do: helping neighbors, reading signs, knowing places, using everyday phrases and showing up day after day.
- **It wobbles** when review words go overdue, and it **can drop a tier** if you disappear for weeks.
- **People speak faster and more naturally as it rises**, in standard Mandarin throughout (no dialect).
- **It must reflect real skill,** never just time spent: every rise comes from using the language.

## Money and items

XP and money stay separate: XP drives your level and is never spent, while 钱 (yuan) is earned alongside it and spent freely.

- **Earning yuan:** from drills, encounters and **odd jobs that are themselves drills**, such as a shift at Master Sun's stall (a timed order-taking listening game) or tutoring Xiao Su in English (translation).
- **Buying is a language task:** every purchase is a short shopping exchange in Chinese.

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| 礼物 Gifts | Raise friendship. Each neighbor has likes and dislikes (Grandma Wang loves 水果, Teacher Zhang loves 茶叶). They refuse politely first (不用不用！) and you insist. |
| 茶 Tea | Restores face during an encounter |
| 公交卡 Bus card | Fast travel between districts |
| 地图 Map | Reveals hidden spots in a district |
| 手电筒 Flashlight | Opens night-time areas |
| 雨伞 Umbrella | Lets you join rain-day events |
| Tickets | The Daming Lake boat, the Qianfo Mountain cable car, the members-only teahouse |
| 红包 Red packets | Given at festivals and for favors. 6, 8 and 88 are lucky; 4 (四, which sounds like 死 “death”) costs face. Used sparingly, since too many is 太客气. |

**Guardrail:** nothing you can buy skips the language. A hint can show pinyin, but you still have to produce the Chinese.

## Getting there from today's app

Six phases, each shippable on its own, with progress carried through every step.

1. **Foundations (invisible):** save data for chapters, clues, notebook pages, yuan, face and local status; every level, story and quest tagged to a district arc.
2. **The notebook:** an opening scene (arrive, inherit the house), the locked notebook, local status beside the animal rank, and “Today in Jinan” replacing today's plan.
3. **District arcs:** the quests regrouped into ten 5-beat arcs at HSK 1, the stories turned into in-world documents, and face hearts added to conversations.
4. **Economy:** yuan, the Quancheng shop, gifts, tea, red packets and odd jobs built from today's drills.
5. **Encounters:** a battle screen built from existing drill types (choice, tones, typing, strokes) with face bars and rivals.
6. **The pixel world:** a walkable pixel map with an avatar, interiors and day/night, on a game engine (Phaser) inside the app. Pixel sprites walk the world; the illustrated portraits stay for conversations, as in Stardew Valley and Fire Emblem.

| Today | In the game |
| --- | --- |
| Levels | Lessons at the university, plus training anytime |
| Review | “Refreshing your memory” (possibly a garden later) |
| Streak and tea cups | Morning tea at home |
| Animal ranks | A companion that grows alongside you |
| Dictionary | Your in-world phone (手机) |
| Silent mode | Stays as it is |

**Content changes:** the 12 HSK 1 stories stay in Jinan; the 10 HSK 2 stories move to Chengdu and the 8 HSK 3 stories to Shanghai, with light rewrites since they star Jinan characters. The quests in districts 5–10, currently HSK 2–3, are rewritten at HSK 1.
